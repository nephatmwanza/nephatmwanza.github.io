Three studies of Zambian climate extremes, deliberately built on one pipeline — same country,
same period, same baseline, same statistics — so that the differences between their results
are real differences in the climate, not artefacts of different choices.

**Rainfall is reorganising without a detectable trend. Temperature is warming unambiguously.
And most climate models cannot reproduce the rainfall measure that matters most.** Together
they say something none says alone: plan for rainfall *variability*, plan for heat as a
*trend*, and treat model projections of dry spells with care.

Code and data are public wherever the sources allow, and each study is written up so the
finding survives being read by someone who is not a specialist.
